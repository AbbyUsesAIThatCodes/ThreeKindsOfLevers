Three Kinds Of Levers

The Middle Tells You the Class

  1. Choose First Class, Second Class, or Third Class.
  2. Find the three labeled parts: Effort is the push or pull you apply. The Fulcrum is the fixed point the lever turns around. The Load is what you want to move.
  3. Click Apply Effort. Watch the load rise. Click again to return to level.
  4. Drag any part or its label along the beam. You can move parts past one another. The class changes when a different part is in the middle.

Look around: drag the table to orbit; scroll or pinch to zoom. Side View lines up the beam. Fit View resets the camera.

Keyboard: Tab to a label, then use the left/right arrows. Or open Move Parts and use its slider and buttons. Reverse Arrangement flips the layout without changing its class.

Force arrows: the teal arrow shows the direction of your effort. The gold arrow shows the load’s downward pull, even when the load is rising.

About the model: the beam and attachments are treated as massless, with no axle friction. Apply Effort demonstrates a controlled lift, rather than calculating acceleration or the size of the needed force. First Class reverses the motion direction. Second and third class move effort and load in the same direction. This is a teaching apparatus, not a calibrated VEX build.
