# Third-party notices

## Shared workshop environment

The classroom, table, cutting mat, cactus, lighting, and camera infrastructure
are adapted from AbbyUsesAIThatCodes/MechanicalAdvantage commit
`f316c49745a394e0dd100ff4e8d01c0ae4431286`, descended from LeverWorkshop.
The lever here uses procedural teaching geometry. No VEX CAD files or private
curriculum packets are distributed in this repository. This is an independent
classroom simulation, not a calibrated or certified VEX assembly.

## Photo-Informed Classroom

`src/classroom/classroom.js`, `layout.js`, and `textures.js` reuse the owner's
ClassroomVirtualization source at commit `1f25638e64861424a52f8bf381cea2a247cfe74e`.
Adaptations include toon materials, desk pairs, door hardware and extinguisher.
The owner's original classroom photos were inspected as private references and
are not redistributed or used as textures. See `docs/CLASSROOM-REFERENCE.md`.
