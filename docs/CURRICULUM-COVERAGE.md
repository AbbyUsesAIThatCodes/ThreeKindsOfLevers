## Assessment Interpretation

Quiz records first-response correctness separately from corrected retries. Skipped items remain unattempted. This is a local practice check, not a validated test, gradebook or mastery certification. A successful retry demonstrates revision after feedback. All progress remains in the current page session; Play arrangement storage preserves the existing key.

The approved audit's teacher-spoken descriptions for steps 21 and 26 are absent, and S/M are incomplete captures. Tutorial wording and the everyday transfer examples (except the directly sourced forearm) are our source-informed teaching design. No full-course, Ohio, NEXT READY or HQSD mastery claim is made.

## Preserved Corrections and Deliberate Gaps

R01 N1 corrects 10 g to 0.0981 N at 9.81 m/s². N3 distinguishes load weight from lifting capacity. This bounded game introduces no numerical mass/force, IMA/AMA, efficiency or acceleration assessment. The model deliberately treats beam/attachments as massless and pivot as frictionless. Digital construction does not replace the physical B/W guides, E2, or G20–G27 evidence.
